# ADR 0002: External actions require creator approval

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-05

## Decision

Afterplay may autonomously research, analyze, plan, and prepare work. Publishing, outreach, spending, account changes, and agreements require explicit creator approval.

## Why

The product should remove operational work without taking unearned authority over the creator's identity, money, relationships, or accounts.

## Consequences

- External adapters consume only approved actions.
- Approval is a behavioral boundary, not interface copy.
- Future scoped automation must be explicitly granted and is outside the prototype.
